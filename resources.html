Warehouse Vocabulary

10 terms every warehouse worker should know before their first week.

  • KPI — Key Performance Indicator. Metrics you are responsible for tracking to ensure your operation is running smoothly.
  • Pick Rate — Units picked per hour. Measures the number of items picked by associates each hour of your operation.
  • Inbound — Product coming into the warehouse. Includes traffic (trailers arriving), receiving (product unloaded and scanned in), and putaway (associates placing product in designated locations).
  • Outbound — Product leaving the facility. Associates pick items, send them to the conveyor belt, then load them into trailers to ship to the customer.
  • Shrink — Product that was lost, damaged, or stolen that the warehouse is held responsible for.
  • Cycle Count — A partial count of inventory performed daily during operations. The goal is to count a small portion each day so all inventory is counted by year end without shutting down the entire facility.
  • WMS — Warehouse Management System. The brain of the warehouse. Controls outbound, inbound, inventory, and traffic operations.
  • Throughput — The volume processed by inbound or outbound operations during a measured time period.
  • Dwell Time — Time when product is not moving. Applies to trucks sitting at the dock or physical product not being processed.
  • FIFO — First In First Out. Inventory that arrives first leaves first. Ensures older stock ships before newer stock.
